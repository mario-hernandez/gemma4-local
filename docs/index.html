Features
Like ChatGPT,
but on your Mac.

No internet needed

Works without WiFi. Use it on a plane, in the middle of nowhere, wherever you want. Nothing leaves your computer.

Fast responses

Responses start instantly and stream at good speed. Not as fast as ChatGPT, but you won't be waiting around.

Understands images and audio

Send it a photo and ask what it sees. Or an audio file and ask for a transcription. It's not just text.

It can "think"

When you ask something hard, it can reason step by step before answering. Like when someone stops to think instead of blurting out the first thing that comes to mind.

Saves your chats

All conversations save automatically. Search something you asked weeks ago. Never lose a good answer.

You control it

Make it more creative or more precise depending on what you need. Controls are right there, no menus to dig through.

Works without internet.

On a desert island, on a plane, at your grandparents' village. If your Mac turns on, your AI works.
